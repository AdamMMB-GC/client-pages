Meta access set-up · 09/11/2026

Set up your Meta business portfolio and give Adam access

You already have a Facebook Page. This page helps you put it in a business portfolio you own, add Instagram, and give Adam access. About 25 minutes.

Before you start

  • Use a computer, not your phone (one step may need the Instagram app).
  • Log in to Facebook with the account that runs your business Page.
  • Have your Instagram username and password handy.
  • About 25 minutes. Tick each step as you go. Close the page any time: your ticks are saved on this device.

The three buttons on every step

  • This button (Open Meta Business Suite, Open Settings): press this first. It opens the exact Meta screen for that step in a new tab, so you never hunt through menus.
  • Meta's own instructions: optional. Opens Meta's official help page for that step, with Meta's own screenshots, if you want to see what the screens look like.
  • Ask Adam: stuck? Press it. It opens an email to Adam with the step already filled in, so he knows exactly where you are.
1

Create your business portfolio

  1. Press the button. It opens Meta Business Suite. Log in with the Facebook account that runs your Page.
  2. Top left, click the drop-down below Home.
  3. Click Create a business portfolio.
  4. Name it Platinum Surface Solutions. Enter your first name, surname and your business email.
  5. Click Create (or Create portfolio). Check your email and confirm it.
Open Meta Business Suite Meta's own instructions Ask Adam
You should now see: Platinum Surface Solutions in the top-left drop-down, and a confirmation email in your inbox.

If the drop-down already shows a portfolio with your business name, you have one. Tick this step and move on.

2

Add your Facebook Page to the portfolio

  1. Press the button. It opens Settings in Meta Business Suite.
  2. In the left menu, under Accounts, click Pages.
  3. Click + Add, then Add an existing Facebook Page.
  4. Type your Page name or paste its web address, click Next.
  5. Tick the terms box, click Add Page, then Done.
Open Settings Meta's own instructions Ask Adam
You should now see: your Page listed under Pages with you as the owner.

If your Page is not offered, it may belong to another business portfolio (for example an old agency). Ask Adam.

3

Make sure Instagram is a professional account

  1. On your phone, open Instagram, go to your profile, tap the menu (three lines), then Settings.
  2. Look for Account type and tools. If you can Switch to professional account, do it and choose Business.
  3. If it already says you have a Business or Creator account, nothing to do.
Instagram's own guide Meta's own instructions Ask Adam
You should now see: 'Professional dashboard' at the top of your Instagram profile.

Personal accounts cannot be added to a business portfolio. This step fixes that.

4

Add your Instagram account to the portfolio

  1. Press the button. It opens Settings.
  2. Left menu, under Accounts, click Instagram accounts.
  3. Click Add, then Add Instagram account.
  4. A window opens: enter your Instagram username and password, click Log in (enter your security code if asked).
  5. Click Confirm.
Open Settings Meta's own instructions Ask Adam
You should now see: your Instagram handle listed under Instagram accounts.

If it says the account is in another portfolio, ask Adam before doing anything else.

5

Turn on two-factor authentication

  1. Press the button. It opens Settings.
  2. In the left menu, select Business portfolio info.
  3. Scroll down to the Business options section.
  4. Select the drop-down menu next to Two-factor authentication and choose Admins only.
  5. Confirm your selection. If Meta asks you to set up two-factor authentication on your Facebook account, follow its prompts.
Open Settings Meta's own instructions Ask Adam
You should now see: Admins only showing next to Two-factor authentication.

If you can't change this setting, Meta may have already turned it on for your portfolio. Check it isn't set to No one, then tick this step and move on.

6

Give My Marketing Broker access (the important one)

  1. Press the button. It opens Settings.
  2. Left menu, under Users, click Partners.
  3. Click Add, then Give a partner access to your assets.
  4. Enter Adam's business portfolio ID: 511250526710324 and click Next.
  5. On the left, click each type you have: Pages, Instagram accounts, and Ad accounts if listed. For each, tick your business and switch on Full control.
  6. Click Assign assets.
Open Settings Meta's own instructions Ask Adam
You should now see: My Marketing Broker listed under Partners, with your Page and Instagram shown as shared.

Can't see Partners in the menu? You may not have full control of the portfolio. Ask Adam, it is a quick fix.

All done?

Press the button so Adam knows to accept the access on his side. Nothing else is needed from you.
